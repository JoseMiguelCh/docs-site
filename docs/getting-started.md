# Getting started

## Your first session

Run `pocket start` to begin a 25-minute session. Pocket Timer counts down in the terminal and rings
when the session ends.

## Taking a break

Run `pocket break` for a 5-minute break. Breaks never count toward your [daily goal](configuration.md#daily-goal).

## Seeing your day

Run `pocket today` to list today's sessions and breaks. See the [FAQ](faq.md#where-is-my-history-stored)
for where the history lives.

## Your streak

Your streak is the number of consecutive days, ending today, with at least one completed session.
Pocket Timer shows it at the start of every session, right after you run `pocket start`.

It calculates the streak from your history file, so you don't have to do anything. Breaks don't count.
See the [FAQ](faq.md#how-is-my-streak-calculated) for the exact rule, and
[where your history is stored](faq.md#where-is-my-history-stored).
