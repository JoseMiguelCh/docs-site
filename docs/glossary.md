# Glossary

The four words you will see most in Pocket Timer.

## Session

A session is a block of focused work. You start one with `pocket start`, and it lasts 25 minutes by default. You can change the length in [Session length](configuration.md#session-length).

## Break

A break is a short rest between sessions. You start one with `pocket break`, and it lasts 5 minutes. Breaks never count toward your daily goal. See [Taking a break](getting-started.md#taking-a-break).

## Daily goal

The daily goal is how many sessions make a full day: `daily_goal = 8`. Only sessions count, not breaks. See [Daily goal](configuration.md#daily-goal) to change it.

## History

Your history is the record of your sessions and breaks. It lives in `~/.pocket/history.jsonl`, one line per session or break, and it stays on your computer. See [Where is my history stored?](faq.md#where-is-my-history-stored) for details.
