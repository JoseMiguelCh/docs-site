# Glossary

These are the four terms you meet most often in Pocket Timer.

## Session

A session is a 25-minute focus period that you start with `pocket start`. You can change its length with `session_minutes = 25`; see [session length](configuration.md#session-length).

## Break

A break is a 5-minute rest that you start with `pocket break`. It never counts toward your daily goal. See [taking a break](getting-started.md#taking-a-break).

## Daily goal

The daily goal is the number of sessions that make a full day: `daily_goal = 8`. See [daily goal](configuration.md#daily-goal).

## History

The history is the record of your sessions and breaks, stored in `~/.pocket/history.jsonl`, one line per entry. `pocket today` lists today's entries. See [where your history is stored](faq.md#where-is-my-history-stored).
